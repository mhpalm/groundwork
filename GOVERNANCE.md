# Governance

For v1, Micah Palmquist is the sole maintainer. The maintainer reviews and merges every change, cuts every release, and runs the per-surface smoke checklist before publishing it.

Anyone may contribute through a pull request. `main` is protected: it requires a code owner review and green CI, including the DCO check.

Decisions about scope, the contract and the overlay format are recorded with a date in `CHANGELOG.md` and in the release notes.
